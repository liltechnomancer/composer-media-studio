# Media Studio product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creators working on Woodhouse projects and other owners of an independent
Studio need to collect existing artwork, create and edit media, assemble clips,
review results, and deliberately publish or reuse finished work. An agent may
assist within a project-scoped grant chosen by the owner.

## Product Purpose

Make useful, product-ready image, video and sound work from an owner's source
material, and make the creative process and evidence understandable. The owner
explicitly wants the Studio used on Woodhouse projects, with real work and
recordings suitable for the sites and sharing. A mockup or passing build alone
is not evidence of those outcomes.

## Positioning

Studio owns its project metadata and media independently of App Composer and
uses explicit provider integrations. Saved assets retain source and lineage;
generation, editing, assembly and publication are distinct operations.

## Operating Context

A creator can enter from App Composer or operate the independent Studio. Work is
organised by project; originals and generation results remain in the library.
Provider jobs can be asynchronous or fail, and the interface must make the
actual state, available recovery and estimated cost clear. Work must be usable
at phone and desktop sizes and through keyboard navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing projects, originals, jobs, published URLs, attribution and
useful features. No publication should happen accidentally. Do not present a
provider estimate as an invoice, a mocked test as a paid generation, or a
partial integration as complete. Follow the project's current README and the
scoped MCP guide for implemented behaviour and access boundaries.

## Brand Commitments

The product is Media Studio, an independent application in the App Composer
family. Keep first-party artwork and source provenance. The owner has explicitly
rejected the earlier janky navigation, colour contrast and general Studio UI;
current pixels are implementation evidence, not blanket design approval.

## Evidence on Hand

The README describes the current architecture and capabilities. Existing public
Studio portfolio items and project-owned assets are real examples; their
individual publication and provenance records are the source of truth. Do not
invent usage, performance, customer or provider-success claims.

## Product Principles

- Preserve and build on the owner's existing work.
- Make the next creative action, cost and outcome clear.
- Support deliberate ownership, sharing and access boundaries.
- Show real work and its evidence in the places it is useful.
- Fix the functioning experience, not just its presentation.
